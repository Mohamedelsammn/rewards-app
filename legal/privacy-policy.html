Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Rewards App collects, why it is collected, and how it is used. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

When you register as a participant, we collect your full name, country, and phone number. Your phone number is used to prevent the same person from entering a single giveaway more than once and is never shown publicly. We also store a device identifier and, if you allow notifications, a push-notification token, so we can tell you when a giveaway you joined has a winner. When you take part in a giveaway we record your participation number and the time you joined.

The app contains no advertising or ad-tracking SDKs and no analytics SDKs, so no advertising identifiers, ad-interaction data, or analytics usage data are collected. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to operate giveaways: creating your participant record, generating and displaying your participation number, selecting and announcing winners, and sending you notifications about giveaways you have joined. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

Giveaway Participation

The app uses an anonymous, device-scoped session to connect your device to your participant record, secured by server-side access rules so that only your device can read or act on your own data. A small identity cache (your name and an internal identifier) is stored securely on your device so you do not need to re-register every time you open the app.

Winner Selection and Notifications

When a giveaway's organizer selects a winner -- at random or manually, as described for that giveaway -- the result is recorded against your participation record. If you allow notifications, we send you a notification when you have won. Winner information (your participation number and, where applicable, your name) may be shown to the organizing administrator and, for the winning entry, announced within the app.

Device and Notification Information

We store a device identifier tied to your participant record so the app can recognize your device, and, if you grant notification permission, a push-notification token used solely to deliver giveaway-related notifications (such as a winner announcement) to your device.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase to host our database, authentication, file storage, and server-side functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments and do not use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Storage and Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, including server-side access rules that restrict every record to the device and account it belongs to, and encrypted storage for your locally cached identity. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep your participant record and participation history for as long as it is needed to operate the giveaways you have joined and to prevent fraud, or until you request deletion.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing mohamed.elsamman.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and delete data we are not legally required to keep.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.
