Delete Your Account

Rewards App

You can request deletion of your Rewards App account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains how.

How to Request Deletion

Account deletion is not yet available directly inside the app. To request deletion, send an email to the address below. If you remember the phone number you registered with, please include it so we can locate your account -- we never ask for a password, since participant accounts in this app don't use one.

Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

Once your request is processed, we permanently delete your participant profile (your name and phone number), your device registration, your giveaway participation history, and your notifications. See our Privacy Policy for what each type of data is used for before it's deleted.

What May Be Retained

Information that we are legally required to retain, or that is necessary to complete a prize you have already won and that is still being fulfilled, may be retained for a limited time even after your account is otherwise deleted.

Processing Time

We aim to process deletion requests within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days of receiving them.
